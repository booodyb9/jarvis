# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## In short

We want this to be a welcoming project for everyone, whatever their experience, background or identity.

**Do:**
- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers.
- Respect different viewpoints, and assume good intent.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what's best for the project and its users.

**Don't:**
- Harass, insult or demean anyone, publicly or privately.
- Use sexualised language or imagery, or make unwelcome advances.
- Publish anyone's private information without their permission.
- Engage in trolling, deliberately derailing discussions, or other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and whenever someone represents the project in public.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour concerns you, contact the maintainers privately at **judejulius12@gmail.com**. All reports will be reviewed promptly and kept confidential. Maintainers may remove comments, commits or other contributions, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose behaviour breaks this code.

The full text of the Contributor Covenant, including the enforcement guidelines, is at https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/.
